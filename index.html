The docket trail

Five stamps.
No shortcuts.

Every job, on either side of Skillchain, moves through the same five checkpoints. Money only moves forward — never sideways, never on a promise.

01
Job is posted

Client or employer drops the job — a fan install, a cleaning run, a delivery. Matched to nearby workers or contractors, usually within the hour.

✓
02
Payment locked in escrow

The naira moves into Skillchain's escrow before anyone leaves for site. Neither side can touch it until the job is confirmed complete.

✓
03
Worker or contractor arrives

A check-in ping fires on arrival — photo, NIN status, timestamp. The client knows exactly who's at the gate before opening it.

✓
04
Proof of work goes on record

A completion photo or short video is logged against the docket. The client reviews it in-app — no argument about what was actually done.

✓
05
Docket is stamped, escrow releases

Once approved, payout hits the wallet instantly — withdrawable to any bank or mobile money account within minutes.
