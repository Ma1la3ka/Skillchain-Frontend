THE CHAIN OF CUSTODY

Five links. No surprises.

Every job follows the same sequence, and every link has to hold before payment moves to the next. Nothing skips ahead.

01
Post the job

Drop a pin, describe the work. Takes under three minutes. We match you to nearby verified artisans, usually within the hour.

02
Lock payment in escrow

Money moves into escrow before any work starts, via Squad. Neither side can touch it until the job is confirmed complete.

03
Artisan travels to site

They head to your pinned location. You're notified as they approach — no waiting in the dark about whether anyone is coming.

04
GPS proof recorded

A ten-second on-site video, GPS locked to that exact moment. Within 100m of your pin, presence is verified. No match, no payout.

✓
Escrow releases automatically

The geofence passes, and payment moves to the artisan's virtual account instantly — no delay, no one skimming a cut in between.

What actually makes this different.

Not a new coat of paint on the same old agency model. Three structural things that change how trust and payment work.

Reputation earned, not self-declared

Every rating is assembled from GPS-confirmed jobs, client reviews, and peer vouches. You can't buy a good score here — you earn it, job by job.

Proof of presence, not a promise

The artisan records video on-site with GPS locked to that moment. If the coordinates don't match your pin, payment doesn't move — no override, no argument.

Payment moves when work is confirmed

Escrow sits untouched until proof passes. Then it moves automatically, in seconds, into the artisan's account. Neither side has to trust the other to follow through.
